# AHA/ACC 2025 High Blood Pressure Guideline: Classification Summary

Source: https://professional.heart.org/en/science-news/2025-high-blood-pressure-guideline/top-things-to-know

## Scope and clinician review

This short project summary is a retrieval corpus excerpt, not a diagnostic or treatment recommendation. Use validated measurements, the full guideline, local protocols, and clinician judgement when evaluating blood pressure.

## Classification framework

The 2025 AHA/ACC framework describes normal blood pressure as systolic below 120 mm Hg and diastolic below 80 mm Hg. It describes elevated blood pressure as systolic 120 to 129 mm Hg with diastolic below 80 mm Hg. Stage 1 is systolic 130 to 139 mm Hg or diastolic 80 to 89 mm Hg; stage 2 is systolic at least 140 mm Hg or diastolic at least 90 mm Hg.

## Measurement and context

Blood pressure classification should be based on appropriate measurements and clinical context. The guideline identifies home blood-pressure monitoring with structured clinical follow-up as useful, and notes limitations in relying on cuffless devices for accurate measurement.
