# ADA Standards of Care 2026: Diabetes Screening and Classification

Source: https://diabetesjournals.org/care/article/49/Supplement_1/S27/163926/2-Diagnosis-and-Classification-of-Diabetes

## Scope and clinician review

This curated project summary supports retrieval demonstrations only. It is not a substitute for the full American Diabetes Association Standards of Care, local policy, clinical judgement, or confirmatory testing. Review the source guideline and the patient's complete context before acting.

## Diagnostic testing context

The ADA describes A1C, fasting plasma glucose, and two-hour plasma glucose during a 75 g oral glucose tolerance test as tests used for screening and diagnosis. In the absence of unequivocal hyperglycemia, abnormal results require confirmation. Test selection and interpretation depend on the clinical context and possible factors that affect A1C or glucose measurements.

## Screening context

The guideline recommends using risk assessment to guide testing for prediabetes and type 2 diabetes. It describes testing adults with overweight or obesity plus relevant risk factors, and beginning screening at age 35 for other adults. Results and patient risk influence when repeat testing is appropriate.
